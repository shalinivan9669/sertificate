Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the training take?

The average completion time is 4-6 hours. However, you can study at your own pace; access is granted for 30 days.

What happens if I fail the final test?

You are granted 3 attempts. If you fail all three, you must re-enroll in the module, but our support team can provide additional guidance.

Is the certificate valid for international projects?

This specific certification is designed for the Republic of Kazakhstan state standards. For international OHSAS standards, please check our Global Catalog.
