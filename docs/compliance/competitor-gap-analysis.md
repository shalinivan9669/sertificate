# Competitor gap analysis

Competitor material is used only as a public product/UX reference. It is not legal evidence and must not be copied.

## KazProfi

Observed strengths:
- Publishes a comparatively visible set of authority/attestation information.
- Broad industrial-safety and adjacent training catalog.
- Makes the legal entity's authorizing documents easier to discover than many training sites.

Gap for OT Center to win:
- Link each course to the exact applicable authority, source and scope rather than merely displaying documents on a separate page.
- Add version history and public evidence status.

## KCSO

Observed strengths:
- Training platform and large course catalog.
- Public pages for licenses/attestations, formats and pricing.
- Strong B2B/remote-learning presentation.

Observed risk:
- Public marketing includes broad “inspection/fine” style assurances. OT Center should not copy guarantee language.

Gap for OT Center to win:
- Make every completion-document claim traceable to regulation + provider authority + approved program + current revision.
- Show transition notices when the law changes instead of leaving stale pages live.

## Kazfactor

Observed strengths:
- Very broad catalog and fast commercial navigation.
- Strong emphasis on rapid issuance/availability.

Observed risk:
- Broad claims such as “official documents”/“admissions” are marketing statements unless a specific legal basis is linked.

Gap for OT Center to win:
- Prefer exact legal terminology over speed claims.
- Expose evidence level and verification route on every course.

## УКК

Observed strengths:
- Wide set of industrial, mining, electrical, fire-safety and machinery training areas.
- Onsite/theory/practice positioning.

Observed risk:
- Some public authority references appear historical and require current-version verification before relying on them.

Gap for OT Center to win:
- Build an authority expiry/version monitor and never present an old commission/attestation as current without a fresh check.

## Product target

OT Center should own the chain:

`NPA → employer requirement → worker/profession → course → approved program → training → knowledge check → protocol → exact document → verification registry → expiry/retraining`

The competitive advantage is auditability, not copied copywriting, design, photos or test banks.
