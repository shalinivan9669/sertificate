# Missing owner inputs

These inputs are required before strict publication gates can pass.

## Legal entity and authority

1. Document proving how the OT Center brand/operator is legally related to ТОО «Аттестационный центр Стандарт» (same operator, contract, authorization, group relationship or other accurate basis).
2. Exact legal operator name and BIN used in offers, invoices, learner contracts and personal-data processing.
3. Names/scans/PDFs of every additional claimed license, attestation, accreditation, permit or certificate.
4. For each document: exact scope, issuer, issue date, validity and official verification route.

## Academic and examination evidence

5. Approved current program and version for each course.
6. Approval order/minutes and responsible approver.
7. Current examination/knowledge-check commission order, members and authority where applicable.
8. Exact learner document template(s), numbering rule, signature/seal rule and reissue/annulment procedure.
9. Retest and appeal procedures actually used by the center.

## Materials and rights

10. Inventory of lectures, slides, videos, tests, forms and checklists.
11. Author/rights basis for each item.
12. Which assets are public, learner-only, printable, downloadable or editable.
13. Version/approval record for internally developed test banks.

## Consents and privacy

14. Approved legal texts for the 12 consent/rule types in `consent-matrix.json`.
15. Personal-data controller/operator details and retention periods.
16. Proctoring/video retention policy.
17. State-registry/ECS integration legal basis and actual integration contract/API when applicable.

## Platform architecture

18. List of actual training-center tenants/legal entities.
19. Role matrix per tenant.
20. Authentication/identity provider choice.
21. Storage/database architecture for tenant isolation, immutable audit log and consent evidence.
22. Public document verification policy and fields safe to expose.
